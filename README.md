# An independent guide to Citi card activation and verification.
Have you recently received a new Citi commercial card and need to confirm its receipt before using it? Understanding the card activation process is an important first step. Eligible cardholders can use the official Cardactivation.citi.com website to complete the required card receipt verification process.

This comprehensive guide explains how Citi card activation works, what information you may need, how to complete the verification process, and what to do if you experience an issue. Whether you are activating a newly issued commercial card or checking the instructions that came with your card, the information below can help you understand the process.
